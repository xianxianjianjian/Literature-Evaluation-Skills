## Candidates

### TOPIC-01
- Type: continuation of unresolved core question
- Topic: Cross-night test–retest reliability of macro- and micro-level PSG sleep EEG metrics
- Core question: Across repeated nights, what reliability range is typically reported for sleep architecture and representative microstructural EEG metrics, and which metrics are stable enough to support a CountingSheep PSG reliability hypothesis?
- Why now: This directly continues the existing reliability project and can provide the empirical basis for the expected ICC/reliability range.
- Link to previous reading: Continues the earlier task of defining cross-night reliability ranges and choosing representative macro/micro indicators.
- Expected evidence: repeated-night PSG studies reporting ICC, correlation, coefficient of variation, Bland–Altman or related reliability metrics.
- Preferred study type: empirical repeated-measures PSG studies; methodological validation studies when directly relevant.
- Method/measurement value: very high.
- Transfer value: very high for hypothesis formulation and indicator selection.
- Priority: 1
- Notes: broad enough to map the field, but may need a later narrower week for specific microstructure metrics.

### TOPIC-02
- Type: current core research problem
- Topic: How many nights are needed to obtain reliable individual sleep EEG phenotypes?
- Core question: How strongly does night-to-night variability limit trait-like interpretation of PSG metrics, and how does reliability change with one versus multiple nights of recording?
- Why now: Directly addresses whether a single-night or multi-night design is sufficient for stable individual-difference inference.
- Link to previous reading: Extends the test–retest question from “which metrics are reliable” to “what recording design is needed for reliability.”
- Expected evidence: repeated-night studies, variance-decomposition studies, generalizability/reliability analyses, first-night-effect work.
- Preferred study type: empirical longitudinal/repeated-night PSG.
- Method/measurement value: high.
- Transfer value: very high for study design and interpretation.
- Priority: 2
- Notes: narrower and more design-oriented than TOPIC-01.

### TOPIC-03
- Type: methodological supplement
- Topic: Cross-night reliability of sleep spindle and slow-wave metrics across detection methods and EEG channels
- Core question: To what extent do algorithm choice, channel selection, and detection parameters change the reliability of spindle/slow-wave measures across nights?
- Why now: Microstructural indicators are attractive for the project but may be especially sensitive to algorithm and channel choices.
- Link to previous reading: Directly follows the earlier need to choose representative, not-outdated micro indicators that CountingSheep can export.
- Expected evidence: automated/manual detector comparisons, channel-specific reliability studies, repeated-night spindle/slow-wave validation studies.
- Preferred study type: methodological empirical studies with repeated PSG nights.
- Method/measurement value: very high.
- Transfer value: high for pipeline choice and QC.
- Priority: 3
- Notes: best if the current decision bottleneck is microstructure rather than the overall reliability hypothesis.

### TOPIC-04
- Type: adjacent direction
- Topic: Trait-like stability and cross-night reproducibility of sleep EEG spectral and aperiodic activity
- Core question: Are spectral power and aperiodic/background EEG parameters reproducible enough across nights to serve as individual-difference markers in sleep research?
- Why now: Aperiodic activity is a newer sleep-EEG direction and may complement conventional macro/micro metrics.
- Link to previous reading: Connects the project's prior interest in non-periodic activity with the reliability framework.
- Expected evidence: repeated-night spectral/aperiodic analyses, test–retest studies, methodological papers on parameterization reliability.
- Preferred study type: recent empirical sleep EEG studies, preferably with repeated nights.
- Method/measurement value: high.
- Transfer value: medium to high depending on CountingSheep export capability.
- Priority: 4
- Notes: more exploratory; should not replace core metrics without evidence of export feasibility and reliability.

### TOPIC-05
- Type: frontier/method infrastructure
- Topic: Reproducibility of automated PSG-derived sleep biomarkers in large or open datasets
- Core question: How are modern automated pipelines evaluating reproducibility, robustness, and cross-dataset stability of derived sleep biomarkers?
- Why now: Could inform later scaling beyond a single reliability analysis and identify current reproducibility practices.
- Link to previous reading: Extends CountingSheep usage from metric extraction toward reproducible analysis pipelines.
- Expected evidence: recent open-dataset validation, automated staging/microstructure pipelines, reproducibility or benchmarking studies.
- Preferred study type: recent empirical benchmarking/validation studies.
- Method/measurement value: high.
- Transfer value: medium for the immediate hypothesis, higher for pipeline design.
- Priority: 5
- Notes: frontier-oriented and less directly tied to the immediate cross-night ICC hypothesis.
