# Zotero Archive Policy

## Purpose

Zotero is the preferred long-term literature archive layer.

## Archive Structure

Recommended organization:

```text
Zotero:
- Main PDF
- Supporting Information
- Translation A
- Deep Reading B

Git:
- Skills
- Policies
- Knowledge records
- Workflow states
```

## Completion Separation

Zotero archive completion is independent from academic workflow completion.

A research task may be academically complete while Zotero synchronization remains pending.

## Integrity Requirements

Before archive completion:

- Verify paper identity.
- Verify attached files.
- Avoid claiming storage before confirmation.
- Record pending actions when synchronization is unavailable.

This prevents false archive states.
