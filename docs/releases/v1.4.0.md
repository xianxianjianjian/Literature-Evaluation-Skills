# Literature Evaluation Plugin v1.4.0

Release date: 2026-09-06

## Outcome

v1.4.0 hardens the formal completion boundary. Existing v1.3 exact-position rendering remains the base, while independent content, DOCX, Zotero, history and terminology gates now prevent a polished but incomplete artifact from being labeled `COMPLETE`.

## Translation A

- `FULL_MIRROR` remains `EXACT_TEXT_FRAME`; no automatic structural downgrade is allowed.
- Language authority is separated from the Version-of-Record PDF geometry authority.
- `translation_integrity.py` compares scientific notation, signs, statistics, units, DOI and software versions and writes `numeric_integrity.json`.
- `figure_text_inventory.jsonl` closes every embedded label through reviewed/rendered/validated states.
- `source_conflicts.jsonl` preserves Main/SI disagreements with `AUD-xxx` links.
- `paper_terminology.csv` is a required evidence-linked exact-package input.

## Deep Reading B

`validate_deep_reading_package.py` enforces the Chinese 0–10 heading schema, required Methods/Results/Discussion subsections, metadata sanitation, OOXML relationships/namespaces, `python-docx` readability, an independent LibreOffice-compatible PDF render, all-page PNGs and reviewed visual-QA flags. Render failure is `PROVISIONAL`, never `COMPLETE`.

## Zotero

`zotero_bridge.py ingest-pdf` makes the PDF-first recognition/verification route explicit. It verifies the recognized parent, collection and PDF child. Metadata-only creation remains an explicit, reasoned fallback and cannot close archive status. Archive closure now requires collection, parent, Main, required SI, A, B and no pending action.

## Knowledge records

- `history_manager.py reconcile-selection` and `reconcile-reading` record old/new values and reasons in append-only `history_corrections.jsonl`.
- `terminology_registry.py` adds evidence add/deprecate/unlink/validate commands and constrains focal-paper TE7 evidence to reported terms.
- `validate_terminology_consistency.py` compares paper choices, the registry/evidence graph and A/B/C artifact use.

## Compatibility

Existing v1.3 structural or incomplete packages remain readable but must not be re-certified as v1.4 complete without the new evidence. Research data is not moved during upgrade.
