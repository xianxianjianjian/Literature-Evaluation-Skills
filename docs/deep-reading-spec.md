# Deep Reading Specification

## Purpose

Deep Reading converts a published paper into an auditable research record rather than a simple summary.

## Core Structure

The standard B document contains:

1. Introduction and theoretical framework.
2. Methods reconstruction.
3. Results and statistical route.
4. Discussion and critical evaluation.

## Evidence Separation

Analysis should distinguish:

- Original author statements.
- Author interpretations.
- Reviewer analysis.
- External evidence.

Important conclusions should link back to sections, figures, tables, or supplementary materials.

## Methods Review

Methods are analyzed from both:

- Participant perspective: task experience and procedure.
- Researcher perspective: design, variables, and statistical decisions.

## Critical Evaluation

The evaluation should discuss:

- Hypothesis support.
- Methodological strengths.
- Limitations.
- Alternative explanations.
- Future improvements.

A complete deep reading requires traceability, not only narrative completeness.
